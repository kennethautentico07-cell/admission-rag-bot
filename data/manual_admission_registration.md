# Admission and Registration Manual
**Academic Year: 2026-2027**
**Document Version:** 1.0

## 1. General Admission Policies
The University is committed to providing equal educational opportunities to all qualified applicants regardless of socio-economic status, gender, religion, or ethnic background. Admission is a privilege, not a right. The University reserves the right to refuse admission to any applicant whose qualifications do not meet the institutional standards or whose records indicate behavior contrary to the institution's core values. All submitted documents become the property of the University Registrar and will not be returned to the applicant.

## 2. Documentary Requirements for Enrollment
To ensure proper evaluation and placement, applicants must submit authentic, original copies of the following documents in a long brown envelope.

### 2.1. Incoming Freshmen
*   **Form 138 (High School Report Card):** Must be the original copy signed by the high school principal. This verifies the completion of the secondary education curriculum and indicates eligibility for college admission.
*   **Certificate of Good Moral Character:** Must be issued by the high school guidance counselor or principal, dated within the last six months. This ensures the applicant has no severe disciplinary records.
*   **PSA Birth Certificate:** One (1) original and two (2) photocopies issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA). This is strictly required to verify the applicant's legal identity and citizenship status for tertiary reporting.
*   **ID Pictures:** Four (4) copies of recent 2x2 ID pictures with a white background and the applicant's name tag.
*   **Medical Clearance:** Issued by the University Clinic, confirming the student is physically fit to undergo their chosen degree program (particularly important for laboratory-heavy courses like BSIT).

### 2.2. Transfer Students (Transferees)
*   **Honorable Dismissal / Transfer Credentials:** Original document from the previous institution permitting the student to transfer.
*   **Transcript of Records (TOR) or True Copy of Grades:** Required for the evaluation and crediting of previously taken subjects. Only subjects with a grade of 85% (or equivalent) and above may be credited, subject to the approval of the Department Head.
*   **Certificate of Good Moral Character:** Issued by the Student Affairs office of the previous institution.
*   **PSA Birth Certificate:** One (1) original and two (2) photocopies.

### 2.3. Returning Students (Returnees)
Students who took a leave of absence for one or more semesters must provide:
*   **Clearance Form:** Signed by the Cashier, Library, and Student Affairs indicating no pending liabilities from their last enrolled semester.
*   **Re-admission Form:** Approved and signed by the University Registrar and the respective Department Head.

## 3. The Five-Step Enrollment Workflow
During the designated enrollment period, applicants must strictly follow the sequential workflow below. 

### Step 1: Document Verification (Registrar's Office - Window 1)
The applicant submits all required documents. The Admission Officer evaluates the completeness and authenticity of the requirements. If complete, the applicant is issued an Examination Permit.

### Step 2: Entrance Examination (Testing Center - Room 102)
The applicant presents the Examination Permit to the proctor and takes the standardized entrance exam. The exam spans two hours and covers Reading Comprehension, Logical Reasoning, and Basic Quantitative Skills. Results are released within 24 hours.

### Step 3: Academic Advising and Subject Plotting (Department Offices)
Upon passing the entrance exam, the student proceeds to their respective Department Head (e.g., the BSIT Coordinator or BSBA Dean). The Department Head evaluates the student's curriculum checklist and plots the approved subjects and class schedules into the Student Information System.

### Step 4: Assessment of Fees (Accounting Office - Window 3)
The student presents the plotted schedule to the Accounting Office. The Assessment Officer generates the official breakdown of tuition, laboratory fees, and miscellaneous fees. Students applying for the Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) must declare their intent here for proper tagging.

### Step 5: Payment and Issuance (Cashier - Window 4)
The student pays the required downpayment or full tuition fee. Upon successful transaction, the Cashier prints the official **Certificate of Registration (COR)**. The student is now officially enrolled and may proceed to the ID Processing section.

## 4. Late Enrollment and Revision of Load
*   **Late Enrollment:** Students enrolling after the official start of classes will be subject to a late enrollment penalty fee of ₱500.00. Late enrollment is only permitted up to two weeks after the commencement of classes.
*   **Adding and Dropping of Subjects:** Students may add or drop subjects within the first two weeks of classes by filling out a Revision of Load (ROL) form, which must be signed by the Academic Adviser and submitted to the Registrar. 
*   **Withdrawal:** Dropping a subject after the two-week period is considered an official withdrawal. The student will be billed for the full cost of the subject, and a mark of "W" (Withdrawn) will appear on their transcript.

## 5. International Student Admission
Foreign nationals seeking admission must submit:
*   Validated Student Visa (9f) or Special Study Permit (SSP) from the Bureau of Immigration.
*   Alien Certificate of Registration Identity Card (ACR I-Card).
*   Authenticated High School scholastic records from their country of origin (Red Ribbon).
*   Proof of English proficiency (e.g., TOEFL or IELTS) if the student's native language is not English.
