Talibon Polytechnic College Admission RAG
 Knowledge Base
A chatbot-ready knowledge base adapted from the official TPC Student Handbook. This version
keeps only information useful for a college admission RAG chatbot and removes unrelated
disciplinary and administrative sections.

--- Page 10 ---
9. Admission Eligibility
Graduates of recognized public/private senior high schools may apply regardless of age, gender,
religion, or race. Applicants are screened by the Admission Committee.

--- Page 11 ---
10. Admission Test
All applicants for degree programs must take the TPC Admission Test consisting of verbal and
performance subtests.

--- Page 12 ---
11. Freshman Admission Requirements
Required documents:
 Admission Test Result
 Form 138/137
 Good Moral Certificate
 PSA Birth Certificate
 Recent ID photos
 Personal Data Sheet
 Brown envelope
 White folder

--- Page 14 ---
13. Continuing Students
Grade slip, examination permit, clinic form, and evaluation form are required before enrollment.

--- Page 15 ---
14. Shiftees, Returnees, Cross Enrollees
Shifting requires signed forms and clearance. Returnees follow current admission policies except
placement examination. Cross enrollees need a cross-registration permit.

--- Page 16 ---
15. Foreign Student Requirements
Alien Certificate of Registration and CHED-certified supporting documents depending on citizenship
status.

--- Page 17 ---
16. Enrollment Policies
Enrollment is valid only during the official registration period. Students become officially enrolled
only after receiving a validated Certificate of Registration (COR).

--- Page 19 ---
18. Adding, Dropping, Withdrawal
Adding and dropping are processed through the Registrar during the allowed period. Withdrawal
charges depend on the week of withdrawal.

--- Page 23 ---
22. Documentary Services
Transcript of Records, Good Moral Certificate, Enrollment Certificate, CAV, Honorable Dismissal,
and other registrar-issued documents have corresponding fees.

--- Page 24 ---
23. Student Rights Relevant to Admissions
Right to quality education, privacy of records, academic freedom, guidance services, and access to
official school documents.

--- Page 25 ---
24. Student Obligations Relevant to Admissions
Maintain academic integrity, follow school rules, respect staff and students, and participate
responsibly in campus life.
